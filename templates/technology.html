Weather Forecast Prediction

Learn how to predict weather conditions for any location

What is it?

AI-powered weather prediction system that forecasts temperature, precipitation, and humidity for any location worldwide.

How to Use:

  1. Select Date: Choose the day, month, and year you want to predict
  2. Choose Location: Either click on the map or enter coordinates manually
  3. Click Predict: Press the "Predict Weather" button
  4. View Results: See temperature (°C), precipitation (mm), and humidity (%)

Tips:

  • Use the interactive map to select your location accurately
  • Predictions work best for dates within the next 30 days
  • Compare multiple locations to plan agricultural activities

NDVI Vegetation Health Analysis

Monitor crop health and vegetation status in real-time

What is NDVI?

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) measures plant health using satellite imagery. Values range from -1 to +1, where higher values indicate healthier vegetation.

How to Use:

  1. Select Location: Click on the map to choose your farm or field
  2. Upload Image (Optional): Upload satellite or drone imagery for analysis
  3. Analyze: Click "Analyze NDVI" to process the data
  4. Review Results: Check vegetation health status, trends, and recommendations

Understanding Results:

  • 0.6 - 1.0: Excellent health (dense, healthy vegetation)
  • 0.4 - 0.6: Good health (moderate vegetation)
  • 0.2 - 0.4: Fair health (sparse vegetation)
  • Below 0.2: Poor health (stressed or no vegetation)

Tips:

  • Monitor NDVI regularly to detect early signs of crop stress
  • Compare values across different areas of your field
  • Use forecasts to plan irrigation and fertilization

Drought Prediction Model

Assess drought risk levels for better water management

What is it?

Advanced AI model that predicts drought severity levels (D0-D4) based on location, date, and climate patterns to help you plan water resources.

How to Use:

  1. Select Location: Click on the map or enter coordinates
  2. Choose Date: Select the date for drought prediction
  3. Predict: Click "Predict Drought Risk"
  4. Review Analysis: Check risk level and follow recommendations

Drought Levels:

  • D0 - Abnormally Dry: Minor impacts, monitor conditions
  • D1 - Moderate Drought: Some crop stress, plan irrigation
  • D2 - Severe Drought: Significant impacts, conserve water
  • D3 - Extreme Drought: Major crop losses, emergency measures
  • D4 - Exceptional Drought: Widespread losses, critical situation

Tips:

  • Check predictions regularly during dry seasons
  • Plan irrigation schedules based on risk levels
  • Follow AI recommendations for water conservation
  • Compare historical data to identify patterns
